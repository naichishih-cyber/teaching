My background

Hi, I'm Naichi SHIH

Chinese and English teacher with a passion for languages and cultures

Originally from Taiwan and now based in Grenoble, I've built my career around teaching, communication and intercultural exchange.

I taught Chinese for several years in the United States, to high school and university students, before continuing my teaching work in France.

Today, I teach Mandarin Chinese and English to learners from all walks of life, including adults, students and professionals.

My background

  • Native Mandarin speaker from Taiwan
  • Master of Arts (M.A.) in Teaching Chinese as a Foreign Language
  • Teaches both traditional and simplified characters
  • Six years of experience teaching Chinese in the United States
  • CELTA certification for teaching English
  • Experience teaching general and business English to adults
  • Experience leading language and cultural activities

My teaching philosophy

I believe learning a language should be rewarding, practical and enjoyable all at once.

My approach emphasizes communication, interaction and confidence. I want every student to be able to progress at their own pace, without being afraid of making mistakes.

I also love using language as a way to explore new topics: culture, society, the economy, travel and working life.

Beyond languages

I'm especially interested in exchanges between Asian and European cultures, and I take part in initiatives that help them discover each other.
