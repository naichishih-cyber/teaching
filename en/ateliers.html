Parent & child workshops in Grenoble, France

A story, a craft, a new language to discover

Warm, friendly workshops for children aged 2 to 8 and their parents: a story in Chinese or English, followed by a craft inspired by the tale.

A gentle, joyful way to spark children's curiosity about another language and culture — through play and making things together.

How a workshop works

  1. 1

    Story time

    An interactive reading in Chinese or English, with gestures, pictures and simple words.

  2. 2

    Craft time

    Children and parents make something together, inspired by the story.

  3. 3

    Take your creation home

    Along with a few new words to use at home.

Why join?

  • Discover Chinese or English from an early age
  • Enjoy some creative family time
  • Spark curiosity about other cultures
  • No prior knowledge of the language needed
